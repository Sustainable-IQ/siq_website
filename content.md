**Status:** Working public-site content draft, 20 October 2026  
**Audience:** Site editor / owner. This file is **not meant to be published as a page**.

## 1. One-sentence value proposition

Sustainable IQ helps organizations make confident environmental decisions and helps people rediscover practical independence through useful goods, informed choices and resources worth keeping.

## 2. Strategic through-line

**Understand more. Depend less.**

Sustainable IQ has two public expressions of one central idea:

- **Advisory:** Make environmental systems legible. Understand the obligation, evidence, funding and available decisions **before** purchasing a solution, accepting a supplier program or signing a contract. SIQ works on the client's side of the table.
- **Market booth:** Sell useful, enduring objects and carefully selected local products that help customers repair, grow, preserve, own and prepare. Explain why each item matters; avoid an appearance of random unrelated goods.

This should be expressed **without** conflating an antique-mall purchase with professional sustainability advice. The shared principles are stewardship, capability and informed decisions.

## 3. Mission (proposed)

Help people and organizations understand the systems shaping their choices, protect what matters, and build lasting practical value.

## 6. Services page outline

**Header:** Better questions. Stronger decisions.

**Lead:** The first step is not buying software, hiring a specialist or accepting a supplier's terms. It's understanding the problem, the evidence and the choices you actually have.

### Proposed capabilities

1. **Obligation & funding scans**: identify potentially relevant requirements, dates and candidate programs; deliver a prioritized decision brief.
2. **GHG inventory systems & QA**: inventory data controls, review, methodologies, quality management and assurance readiness.
3. **Supplier & Scope 3 readiness**: supplier engagement, data governance, value-chain interventions and insetting program design.
4. **Packaging & EPR navigation**: requirement exposure screening, cross-jurisdictional mapping, source-governed tools, evidence registers and action plans.
5. **Targets, claims & assurance readiness**: substantiation, documentation, management review and gap identification.
6. **Owner-side project scoping & oversight**: water, waste, resilience and procurement decision support for local governments and operators; not licensed engineering.

### Audience distinctions

- **Companies and suppliers**: customer data requests, packaging, carbon and evidence systems.
- **Consultancies and programs**: senior technical judgment and fractional QA capacity.
- **Communities and operators**: local obligations, water/waste, procurement, grants and client-side control.

### Engagement approach

1. Understand the actual problem and identify evidence.
2. Structure priorities, scope and pathway.
3. Equip the client with a deliverable that makes decisions safer, clearer or more controlled.

**Service boundary:** No licensed engineering, legal, tax or construction services. Regulations and financial incentives require up-to-date verification for the client's jurisdiction and situation. Avoid unverified pricing and promises of results.

## 7. Booth page outline

**Proposed heading:** Useful things. Lasting freedom.

**Core description:** A small collection with a bigger idea: buy less, own better, repair what you can, grow something useful and keep the things you love.

**Venue:** Time Travelers Market, 1305 W. Walnut Street, Rogers, AR; 10 × 10 ft, under construction / opening soon as of 20 October 2026. Do not publish an invented opening date or hours.

### Illustrative (not live) merchandise categories

| Theme | Example goods | Value proposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Repair | Vintage hand planes, hand drills, chisels, sharpening stones | Useful tools that can be maintained and repaired |
| Grow | Mint, lemon balm, stevia, herbal plants, garden starts, sunchokes | Grow useful things and learn cultivation |
| Preserve | Ball jars, vintage kitchenware, manual tools, teapots and cups | Prepare, store and preserve with long-life equipment |
| Make | Culinary teas, dried botanicals, infused oils, compliant botanical goods | Locally grown ingredients and transparent craft |
| Own | Vinyl, CDs, inspected players | Own music rather than rent access |
| Reuse | Natural-fiber vintage cotton and linen clothing | Choose quality garments with further useful life |
| Prepare | Compact emergency goods and water filters | Practical resilience without sensationalism |
| Seasonal | Flowers, special plants and premium stems | Seasonal, locally grown goods when available |

### Merchandising story

Theme cards: **Repair / Grow / Preserve / Own**. Each item should have signage about what it is, why it is useful and its condition/limitations. In the longer term, compare sell-through and profit per square foot by category.

### Truthfulness requirements

- Mark categories and example imagery as **illustrative**, not actual available inventory.
- Do not promise medicinal outcomes from balms, herbs or oils.
- Confirm Arkansas food/plant/cosmetic product rules, labels and venue policy for each category before sale.
- Do not imply all old tools or used electronics are restored, tested or safe unless that individual item actually is.
- Confirm whether flowers and seasonal products will be sold at the mall or another venue.
