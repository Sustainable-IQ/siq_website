Selected capabilities

Useful judgment.
Documented outcomes.

From a focused assessment to fractional senior expertise, the work is shaped by the decision in front of you. Services are scoped individually.

01 / Client-side intelligence

Obligation & funding scans

Understand which requirements apply, which do not, what deadlines matter, and which funding or program pathways merit investigation. Leave with a prioritized action brief.

02 / Evidence quality

GHG inventory systems & QA

Build or strengthen quality-managed greenhouse gas inventory processes, review methodologies and controls, and prepare better evidence for assurance and decisions.

03 / Value-chain performance

Supplier & Scope 3 readiness

Translate supplier requests into practical information needs, improve data governance, and design defensible value-chain interventions and insetting programs.

04 / Producer obligations

Packaging & EPR navigation

Identify potential extended producer responsibility exposure, map requirements by jurisdiction and build source-governed registers, evidence trails and action plans.

05 / Substantiation

Targets, claims & assurance readiness

Review climate targets and environmental claims against the evidence needed to support them. Clarify gaps before they become reputational or verification problems.

06 / Owner-side decisions

Project scoping & oversight

Help cities, utilities, districts and organizations frame water, waste or resilience projects, structure procurement questions and maintain a clear client-side decision record.

Service descriptions are examples of possible scopes, not guarantees of regulatory outcomes, certifications, approvals, or funding awards.
